Omelette Recipe

A delicious breakfast classic made in minutes.

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

    1. Beat the eggs in a bowl.
    2. Melt the butter in a hot pan.
    3. Pour the eggs in and cook.
    4. Gently slide the omelette out of the pan directly onto a warm plate. Serve immediately!
